Xenon54 Universal Tracker

A private, local-first app that keeps every part of your day in one place.

Xenon54 Universal Tracker is a mobile app that lets you track your trading killzones, spending, quick notes, groceries, workouts and body metrics — each in its own focused tab. Everything works offline and stays on your device, with optional private backup and multi-device sync to the hidden app folder of your own Google Drive.

Private by design

There is no backend server. We do not collect accounts, analytics, telemetry, or location data. Everything you enter is stored locally on your device.

  • No sign-up required to use the app.
  • No ads, no trackers, no third-party analytics.
  • Voice recordings stay on-device and are excluded from sync.
  • Reset or uninstall wipes your local data at any time.

Optional Google Drive backup

When (and only when) you sign in with Google from the Profile screen, Xenon54 Universal Tracker can back up and sync your data to your own Google Drive.

🔒

Your account only

Backups are written to your Drive's hidden appDataFolder — a private area only this app can see. We have no access to them.

🗝️

Minimal access

Xenon54 Universal Tracker requests a single scope, drive.appdata. It cannot see, modify, or share any of your other Drive files.

🔄

Multi-device sync

Sign in on another device with the same account to sync your enabled sections. Sign out to stop and revoke access.
