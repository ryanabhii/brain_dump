Tracker by ryanabhii

Tracker by ryanabhii is a local-first personal organiser for Android. It lets you log killzones, track trades and performance metrics, manage household expenses, capture voice brain-dumps (transcribed on-device), and set reminders — entirely offline, with no backend server. When you optionally sign in with Google, the app uses the Google Drive drive.appdata scope to read and write backup files in a private hidden folder in your own Google Drive — invisible to other apps, inaccessible to the developer.

Local-First Personal Organiser

All your data stays on your device. Sync privately between your own devices via Google Drive. Zero analytics. Zero backend. Zero compromise on privacy.
